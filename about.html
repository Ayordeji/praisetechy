About

I’m Ayodeji Praise Eluyemi, the person behind Praise Techy. I design and build websites for businesses, founders, and e-commerce brands. I’m also a proud Chelsea fan, even when that takes patience.

Good design should make things easier.

A website should help people understand what you offer, trust your business, and take the next step. That’s what I work toward, whether I’m building a new site, improving an online store, or fixing a website that isn’t doing its job.

I started with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript and went on to work with React, WordPress, Framer, and custom code. I begin each project by understanding what the business needs, what visitors need, and what the team will need to manage after launch.

Over the past six years, I’ve delivered 87+ websites. Some needed a complete build. Others needed a clearer design, faster pages, a better checkout, or custom features. I bring design, development, and practical business thinking to each one.

I studied Electrical and Electronics Engineering at Obafemi Awolowo University. That background taught me to look closely at how things work, find the source of a problem, and build a solution that holds up beyond launch.

When I’m away from my desk, you’ll probably find me playing FIFA on my Xbox, watching Chelsea, cheering for Ronaldo, or trying out a design tool I said I’d only open for five minutes.

Who I Work With.

I work with businesses, founders, e-commerce brands, and agencies that want their websites to do more for them.

We’re a good fit if:

  • You’re ready to launch a website and need help turning your ideas into a clear plan.
  • Your current website looks dated, loads slowly, or is difficult to manage.
  • Visitors are reaching your site but aren’t enquiring, booking, or buying.
  • Your store needs a better shopping experience or custom functionality.
  • Your team needs a reliable design and development partner.

Whether you’re starting from scratch or improving what you already have, I’ll help you work out what the website needs to do and build around that goal.
