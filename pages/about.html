01 About Praise

About

I'm Ayodeji Praise Eluyemi, a designer, developer, and proud Chelsea fan.

Some say good design makes things look nice. I think it makes life easier.

My process blends design, strategy, and development into one smooth experience: where creativity meets performance, and where "looking good" becomes "converting visitors into customers."

My journey started with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript before moving on to React, learning how to make ideas come alive with code. These days, I focus on WordPress and Framer, building high-performance, conversion-driven websites for brands that want to grow, not just exist online.

The difference in my work? Speed and strategy. I deliver 90%+ performance scores, sub-3-second load times, and conversion-focused architecture. Because a beautiful website that loads in 30 seconds and doesn't convert is just an expensive business card. And expensive business cards don't pay the bills.

When I'm not designing websites for Businesses, Founders, and E-commerce Brands, you'll probably find me playing FIFA on my Xbox (and yes, always picking Chelsea), cheering for Ronaldo, or exploring a new design tool I promised myself I'd "just test for five minutes."

I studied Electronic and Electrical Engineering at Obafemi Awolowo University, but somewhere between circuits and code, I discovered my passion for design: the part where ideas turn into experiences that connect with people and drive business results.

My clients are dreamers, doers, and builders who know their websites are more than just pixels on a screen. They're revenue generators. Lead magnets. Credibility builders. Together, we turn strategy into style, browsers into buyers, and websites into growth tools that actually move the needle.
