How I built a high-converting, mobile-first fashion storefront for Emily Sparkles with instant checkout & sub-1.3s load speed.

01 The Brief

Emily Sparkles is a trendsetting fashion and lifestyle apparel brand delivering contemporary collections to style-conscious customers worldwide. Their store needed a high-performance, mobile-first shopping experience that highlights seasonal lookbooks, streamlines product variant selection, and reduces mobile cart abandonment.

01

Create an editorial luxury storefront aesthetic that elevates product photography and brand allure.

02

Implement lightning-fast mobile product pages with instantaneous size and color swatch switching.

03

Reduce mobile checkout friction with automated express payment wallets (Apple Pay, Google Pay).

04

Achieve sub-1.3s page load speeds and 96+ Lighthouse mobile performance.

02 The Solution

Engineered a bespoke, ultra-lean e-commerce frontend with predictive search, optimized media delivery pipelines, high-converting product card drawers, and frictionless 1-click cart slideouts.

01

Interactive seasonal lookbooks with direct "Shop the Look" multi-product purchase triggers.

02

Instant slideout cart with dynamic free shipping progress bar and cross-sell recommendations.

03

Optimized WebP/AVIF asset pipelines ensuring crystal-clear fashion imagery with zero lag.

04

+135% increase in mobile add-to-cart conversions within 45 days of launch.
