01 The Challenge

Crevian Studios needed an authoritative, design-forward agency presence to showcase their ability to deliver world-class digital products for scaling brands. Key project goals were to:

01

Structure a clean hierarchy showcasing core agency capabilities: Product Design, UI/UX, Webflow & WordPress Development, and Brand Strategy.

02

Build high-impact visual case study displays with smooth scroll animations to highlight agency client work.

03

Create friction-free project inquiry and consultation booking funnels to convert high-ticket business leads.

04

Achieve top-tier performance benchmarks, responsive fidelity across mobile screens, and clean technical SEO markup.

02 The Solution

I designed and built a sleek, conversion-engineered WordPress website using bold modern typography, interactive client proof modules, and streamlined lead generation workflows.

01

Crafted an ultra-modern dark aesthetic featuring crisp typographic scales, subtle ambient glows, and engaging micro-interactions.

02

Engineered modular project showcase grids allowing the studio to publish new client transformations effortlessly.

03

Integrated smart lead qualification forms with automated CRM routing and discovery call scheduling.

04

Optimized asset loading, caching, and semantic HTML structure to deliver sub-2s initial paint times globally.
