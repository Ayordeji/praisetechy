E-Commerce & Fashion

Emily Sparkles

How I turned a dropshipping vision into a full brand experience and seamless WordPress store

01 The Challenge

Emily Sparkles started as an idea to make trendy fashion and accessories accessible through dropshipping. The client came to me through a referral, ready to build a full brand from scratch. I created the logo, visual identity, and a functional WooCommerce store built to convert visitors into customers.

01

Create a cohesive brand guide and logo from scratch to give the store an identity.

02

Build a WordPress/WooCommerce store that handles dropshipping (AliExpress products), product uploads, and inventory flows.

03

Integrate secure payment methods and optimize checkout flow so sales don’t drop off.

04

Implement SEO fundamentals to help the new site start ranking in a competitive fashion + accessories niche.

02 The Solution

I handled the project from start to finish — building the brand foundation, designing the website, developing the store, and optimizing it for performance and search visibility.

01

Designed the logo, color palette, and brand guide to set a consistent tone across all touchpoints.

02

Developed the WooCommerce store, imported products from AliExpress, and categorized them for easy browsing.

03

Integrated secure payment methods and simplified the checkout flow to reduce friction.

04

Conducted SEO research, implemented meta descriptions, and optimized site structure to improve search performance.

Engineering Process

This category details the step-by-step approach taken during the project, including research, planning, design, development, testing, and optimization phases.

Phase 01 01

Research & Planning

Analyzed fashion dropshipping trends and competitor pricing to identify a profitable niche. Outlined product categories and mapped the user journey from homepage to checkout.

Phase 02 02

Design & Prototyping

Created a simple and stylish brand identity system. Prototyped the site layout with focus on clarity, accessibility, and fast browsing experience across devices.

Phase 03 03

Development & Implementation

Built the site using WordPress and WooCommerce, configured dropshipping plugins, uploaded and optimized products, set up payment integrations, and finalized SEO settings before launch.
