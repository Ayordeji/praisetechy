No Border Thoughts

How I crafted a serene, intuitive digital sanctuary and booking platform for transformative coaching, Akashic records, and energy healing

See live site ↗ See live site ↗
01 The Challenge

No Border Thoughts required a calming, spiritually grounded digital space to introduce deep healing modalities to clients across Canada and internationally. The goals included:

01

Clearly differentiating complex healing services like Family Constellations, Energy Healing, and Akashic Records.

02

Designing a soothing, organic visual atmosphere that fosters safety, openness, and trust before booking.

03

Building an automated, friction-free appointment scheduling system with timezone detection and payment integration.

04

Optimizing local search engine visibility in Canadian wellness markets and ensuring responsive performance.

02 The Solution

I designed and developed a serene, minimalist WordPress website that turns complex spiritual modalities into inviting, intuitive client journeys.

01

Curated an earthy, calming color palette, elegant typography, and spacious layouts that put visitors in a relaxed mindset.

02

Crafted dedicated service landing pages with FAQ accordions, preparation guides, and client testimonials.

03

Integrated direct session booking and intake form workflows to streamline client onboarding.

04

Implemented technical on-page SEO schema, metadata, image compression, and mobile navigation enhancements.
