Prime Global Initiatives

How I rebuilt a modern, accessible web portal for an NGO empowering underserved communities through education, healthcare, and sustainable development

See live site ↗ See live site ↗
01 The Challenge

Prime Global Initiatives needed a transparent, inspiring online hub to represent their humanitarian campaigns, educational outreach, and health drives. The core technical requirements were to:

01

Re-architect confusing program listings into distinct initiatives: Back-to-School Drives, Community Health, and Youth Mentorship.

02

Implement frictionless donation pathways and volunteer sign-up funnels with global payment support.

03

Create an interactive impact storytelling layout to publish field reports, donor updates, and photo galleries.

04

Deliver WCAG-compliant accessibility, rapid page load speeds on low-bandwidth mobile networks, and enterprise security.

02 The Solution

I delivered an accessible, emotion-driven WordPress web portal designed to inspire trust, drive donor conversions, and spotlight grassroots community impact.

01

Designed a clean, compassionate visual layout featuring warm typography, high-contrast readability, and authentic field imagery.

02

Integrated secure online donation processing with one-time and recurring donation options.

03

Built dedicated landing pages for annual campaigns like the Back to School Drive with progress indicators.

04

Hardened WordPress security, configured automated offsite backups, and achieved a 95+ Google PageSpeed score.
